How Cross-Pollination Works:

  1. An SRE builds a script for Horizontal 1.
  2. Engineers in other verticals discover it in the catalog.
  3. They submit a "Migration & Adoption Request".
  4. A new linked card enters the Idea Funnel as an approved derivative backlog item.
  5. Variables & secrets are parametrized for the new target app.
